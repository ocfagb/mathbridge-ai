About MathBridge

MathBridge helps grade 6–8 students practice one-step equations. When you make a mistake, it tries to figure out which mistake and gives a hint about that — without giving away the answer.

Privacy

  • No accounts, names, emails, or cookies.
  • Your answers stay in this browser tab and disappear when you close it.
  • No ads and no tracking.

About the hints

Answers are checked by the app's math engine, not by AI. Hints come from a mistake guide written and checked by our team.
